\subsection*{Padma}

\subsection*{Travel Log — A Fictional History}

2018

\subsection*{Kyoto, Japan}

Studied abroad for a semester, spent every free weekend cycling to a different temple, never did get used to the humidity. 2019

\subsection*{Jaipur, India}

Went home for a cousin's wedding, stayed an extra month to shadow a block-printing workshop in the old city. 2020

\subsection*{Grounded}

Learned to make dosas from scratch instead. Small consolation. 2021

\subsection*{Tbilisi, Georgia}

First trip once borders reopened, chosen almost at random off a map. Fell hard for the wine cellars and the polyphonic singing. 2022

\subsection*{Patagonia, Chile \& Argentina}

Trekked the W circuit in Torres del Paine, crossed into Argentina by bus, got caught in a whiteout near El Chaltén. 2023

\subsection*{Marrakech, Morocco}

Got lost in the souks on purpose, three separate times, and negotiated for a rug she still hasn't shipped home. 2024

\subsection*{Kerala, India}

A slow month on a houseboat through the backwaters, learning to cook with her grandmother's neighbor. 2025

\subsection*{Faroe Islands}

Chased waterfalls and sheep in equal measure, stayed in a converted boathouse with no wifi and no regrets. 2026 Central Asia (in progress) Planning the Silk Road overland — Samarkand, Bukhara, and Khiva — still working out visas for Turkmenistan.
